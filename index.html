🔐 ABOUT CYBERSECURITY

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, phones, networks, accounts and personal information from unauthorized access, attacks and misuse.

Why does it matter to students?

Students use smartphones, laptops and online services every day. They may use social media, online classes, gaming platforms, digital payments and cloud services.

Cybersecurity awareness helps students identify suspicious activity and make safer decisions online.

  • ✓ Protect personal information
  • ✓ Secure online accounts
  • ✓ Avoid scams and phishing
  • ✓ Protect devices from malware
  • ✓ Use social media safely

🚨 COMMON CYBER THREATS

Know What You're Facing

Understanding how threats work makes it easier to recognize warning signs and protect yourself.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails or messages that try to steal information.

Warning: Urgent requests and suspicious links.
🦠

Malware

Software designed to harm or misuse a device.

Warning: Unknown downloads or unusual device behavior.
🔐

Ransomware

Malicious software that can restrict access to files.

Protection: Keep important files backed up.
🌐

Fake Websites

Websites designed to imitate legitimate services.

Warning: Strange URLs or unexpected login pages.
💰

Online Scams

False offers or requests intended to deceive users.

Warning: Promises of prizes or urgent payments.
🪪

Identity Theft

Misuse of someone's personal information.

Protection: Limit the information shared online.
💬

Cyberbullying

Harassment or harmful behavior through digital platforms.

Protection: Block, report and tell a trusted person.
🧠

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information.

Warning: Someone pressuring you to act quickly.
👨‍💻

Account Hacking

Unauthorized access to an online account.

Protection: Use unique passwords and MFA.
📶

Unsafe Public Wi-Fi

Public networks may not provide strong security.

Protection: Avoid sensitive activities on unsecured networks.

🚨 IF YOU ARE HACKED

Stay Calm and Take Action

If you think an account or device has been compromised, follow these basic steps.

01

Stay Calm

Don't panic or continue interacting with suspicious messages.

02

Secure the Account

Change the password using a safe device if possible.

03

Enable MFA

Turn on multi-factor authentication.

04

Check Activity

Review recent logins and account activity.

05

Report It

Contact the service provider and use official reporting channels.

06

Tell Someone

Inform a trusted parent, teacher or institution when appropriate.
